The intro on Tilde's welcome page (LaTeX ignores everything after
\end{document}): its source is shown next to a picture of its PDF,
main.png. CC0-1.0, like the examples. Keep its lines short (38
characters at most), so they do not wrap there. To change it, compile it
in Tilde, render page 1 at 300 dpi with Ghostscript, crop the printed
part with the same margin on every side (60 pixels), and scale it to 640
pixels wide.
